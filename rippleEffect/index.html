Thank you for checking out my portfolio.

I have a lot of experience in sound design for music for the concert hall, trained in the lineage of Musique concrète and acousmatic traditions. What follows on this page are some recent pieces that demonstrate my facility on sound design.

If you have any questions, please feel free to reach out to me.

Sublimation

For this piece of music I recorded sounds around my apartment, it was during a COVID lockdown, then I ran those sounds through a variety of Pure Data patches. Once I brought these sounds into the DAW, in this case Logic, I began to work on the sound through industry standard plugins.


Usynlig

This piece was constructed through using MIR to index ~65,000 audio files and is a demonstration of a more concrète approach to sound design, instead of more traditional DSP approaches. The main force behind this piece was accomplished by software I wrote to deal with the challenge of working with such a large amount of audio files required for this type of composition.


vizual

This piece was written to be viewed through an oscilloscope so the back and forth between sound design for audio output and for visual output was the driving force of this piece. The synthesis was done in Max/MSP using GenExpr.
