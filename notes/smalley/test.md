# DEFINITIONS
## ONSETS
departure: the action of leaving, especially to start a journey
emergence: the process of coming into view or becoming exposed after being concealed
anacrusis: 'delta' but also one or more unstressed notes before the first bar line of a piece or passage
attack: an aggressive and violent action against a person or place
upbeat: an unaccented beat preceding an accented beat
downbeat: an accented beat, usually the first of the bar
## CONTINUATIONS
passage: the act or process of moving through, under, over, or past something on the way from one place to another
transition: the process or a period of changing from one state or condition to another
prolongation: extension of the spatial length of something
maintanence:  process of maintaining or preserving someone or something
statement: the occurrence of a musical idea or motive within a composition
## TERMINATION
arrival: the emergence or appearance of a new development, phenomenon, or product
disappearance: the process or fact of something ceasing to exist or be in use
closure: a sense of resolution or conclusion at the end of an artistic work
release: allow or enable to escape from confinement; set free
resolution: the passing of a discord into a concord during the course of changing harmony
plane: a level of existence, thought, or development
## MOTION AND GROWTH
### UNI
ascent: a climb or walk to the summit of a mountain or hill
plane: a level of existence, thought, or development
descent: an action of moving downward, dropping, or falling
### RECIPROCAL
parabola:
oscillation: movement back and forth at a regular speed
undulation: the action of moving smoothly up and down
### CYCLIC
rotation: the action of rotating around an axis or center
spiral: winding in a continuous and gradually widening (or tightening) curve
spin: rapid turning or whirling motion
### CENTRIC
vortex: a mass of whirling fluid or air, especially a whirlpool or whirlwind
pericentrality: arranged around a center
centrifugal motion: apparent outward force on a mass when it is rotated
### BI/MULTIDIRECTIONAL
agglomeration: a mass or collection of things
dissipation: to spread thin or scatter and gradually vanish
dilation: the act or action of enlarging, expanding, or widening
contraction: to draw together so as to become diminished in size
divergence: a drawing apart (as of lines extending from a common center)
convergence: independent development of similar traits or features (as of body structure or behavior) in unrelated or distantly related species or lineages
exogeny: the origins of existence of self, or the identity of self, emanating from, or sustaining, outside the natural or influenced realm
endogeny: processes that originate from within a living system such as an organism, tissue, or cell
### Spectral Space
canopy: above 5,000Hz
center: 300Hz and 5,000Hz
root: below 300Hz
