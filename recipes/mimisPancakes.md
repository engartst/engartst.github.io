# Mimi's Pancakes

A family pancake recipe

## Ingredients

### Dry

- 1 cup of flour (self-rising or plain)
- 1.5 tsp. baking powder
- 0.5 tsp. salt

### Wet

- 1 cup of milk
- 1 egg (beat it up a little bit before, ya know)
- 3 tbsp. neutral oil (don't use olive)
- 1 tbsp. maple syrup

## Directions

1. Mix the **dry ingredients** together, ya know you kinda stir it up so it's mixed in.
2. Mix **egg** and **milk** to break the yolk.
3. Add **egg/milk** to the **dry ingredients** (put the wet to the dry).
4. Measure **oil** before the **honey** (so that the honey doesn't stick) add them and stir into batter.
5. Stir it up and if it's too thick, thin with a little milk.
6. Fry them in a pan (greased). When it gets bubbles on the top, it is time to flip it.
