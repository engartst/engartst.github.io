Black Bean and Shrimp Dip

A fun dip for fun parties, from the kitchen of Connie Engart

Ingredients

Sauce

  • Juice from 2 Limes (or more if small)
  • About 1/2 cup Olive Oil (Extra Virgin)
  • A couple of cloves of Garlic (minced or grated fine)
  • Salt and Pepper to taste

The Rest of the Ingredients

  • 12 oz. or more of Shrimp
  • 3-4 Green Onions (or what Connie calls spring onions)
  • 2 ears of Corn (or canned corn)
  • 1/2 of a bunch of Cilantro
  • 1 large Bell Pepper (I only used yellow, orange and red peppers, Janet used jalapeÒos)
  • 1 small package of cherry tomatoes
  • 1 Avocado
  • 2 cans of Black Beans

Directions

  1. Stir these sauce ingredients together in a large bowl.
  2. Cooked shrimp and cut into small pieces. Put these in the sauce to let marinate while fixing the other ingredients.
  3. Cut green onion into small "rings".
  4. Cook corn and cut off cob (or you can use canned corn).
  5. Cut up cilantro fine.
  6. Cut peppers into small pieces.
  7. Cut the cherry tomatoes into quarters.
  8. Cut the avocado into small pieces.
  9. Rinse and drain the black beans.
  10. You just mix each of these ingredients into the large bowl with the sauce and shrimp. Stir them up well and serve or refrigerate until ready to serve
