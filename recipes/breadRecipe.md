# Bread Recipe

A simple bread recipe

## Ingredients

- 1 package (1/4 ounce) active dry yeast
- 2-1/4 cups warm water (110° to 115°)
- 3 tablespoons sugar plus 1/2 teaspoon sugar
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 6-1/4 to 6-3/4 cups bread flour

## Directions

1. In a large bowl put **1/4 ounce** *yeast*, **1/2 teaspoon** sugar, **2-1/4 cups** *warm water*. Let stand until bubbles form on the surface.
2. Add **3 tablespoons** *sugar*, **1 tablespoon** *salt*, and **3 cups** *flour*. **Whisk** together.
3. Add **2 tablespoons** *oil*.
4. Add the remaining *flour* (**3-1/2 cups**) **1/2 cup** at  a time.
5. Dump on a floured surface and knead for **8-10 minutes**.
6. Put in a oiled bowl then flip it over so that both sides get oiled.
7. Cover and let rise until doubled, **1-1/2 to 2 hours**.
8. Punch dough.
9. Dump on a floured surface and divide in half.
10. Put in oiled loaf pans.
11. Cover and let rise until doubled, (**1 to 1-1/2 hours**).
12. Bake at **375°** for **30-35 minutes**.
13. Turn off the oven and crack the oven door and let the bread cool for **20 minutes**.
