Baked Spaghetti

Ingredients

Red Sauce

  • 1 lb 97/3 Extra Lean Ground Beef or Ground Turkey
  • 24 oz jar Pasta Sauce
  • 1/2 Tbsp Chili Powder
  • 1/2 Tbsp Garlic Powder
  • 1/2 Tbsp Onion Powder
  • 1 tsp Ground Cumin
  • Salt and Pepper to taste

White Sauce

  • 16 oz Low Fat Cottage Cheese
  • 1/2 cup Fat Free Fairlife Milk
  • 1/2 cup Grated Parmesan Cheese
  • 4 Garlic Cloves

Other

  • 15 oz Barilla Protein Spaghetti
  • 1/2 cup Reduced Fat Shredded Cheese

Steps

  1. Brown and drain meat, add sauce, and spices.
  2. In blender: combine all ingredients.
  3. Cook pasta.
  4. Put pasta in a baking dish, mix in white sauce, add red sauce on top, and then cover in shredded cheese.
  5. Bake at 350* for 10-15 minutes.
  6. Divide into 6 parts.
