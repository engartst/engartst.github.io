# Connie's Banana Pudding

A family banana pudding recipes

## Ingredients:

- 6-7 bananas (big bananas, not little bananas)
- A box of vanilla wafers
- 1 can of sweetened condensed milk
- 1 large can of evaporated milk
- 8 oz. sour cream
- 1 pack of French Vanilla Instant Pudding Mix
- 1 large Cool-Whip
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract

## Directions: (aka whatcha do is)

1. Mix all your ingredients up except for the **bananas, wafers**, and **instant pudding** (once you add your pudding it will stiffen up). N.b. only add a cup of the **Cool-Whip**, save the rest to go on top.
2. Dump the box of **cookies** into a 9"x13" pan.
3. Cut your **bananas** over those in little slices.
4. Add the **instant pudding mix** right before you are ready to do the next step.
5. Pour the **pudding mix** over the **bananas** and **wafers**.
6. Top with the rest of the **Cool-Whip**.
7. Refridgerate.
