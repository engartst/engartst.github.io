Dee Engart's Macaroni & Cheese

A family Macaroni and Cheese recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of uncooked elbow macaroni
  • 2 cans of cream of mushroom soup (just the little cans)
  • 2 small cans of mushrooms (sliced)
  • 8 oz, sour cream
  • Whole onion (chopped)
  • Some milk (to thin)
  • 8 oz. shredded cheese
  • Bread crumbs
  • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook the macaroni. Chop the onion. Pre-heat oven 275* F.
  2. Mix the soup, milk, mushrooms, sour cream, onion, cheese.
  3. Add macaroni to mixture.
  4. Put it in a casserole dish and top with breadcrumbs and paprika.
  5. Bake for 30 minutes or something. Maybe 40, I don't remember.
