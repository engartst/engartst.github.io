Linda's Mac and Cheese

A Chapel Hill favorite recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of macaroni
  • 1 quart water
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • olive oil
  • 4 cupls whole fat milk
  • 2 sticks butter
  • 0.5 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 pound block American cheese
  • 0.25 pound shredded Cheddar cheese
  • 0.25 pound shredded Monterey Jack cheese
  • 0.25 box Frosted Flakes cereal
  • 0.5 cup Texas Pete hot sauce

Directions

  1. Move oven rack to 2nd highest position in oven.
  2. Preheat oven, 350* F.
  3. Boil water in a large stock pot.
  4. Add macaroni and salt to boiling stock pot. Cook 7 minutes, stirring ever 3.
  5. Strain noodles in colander, coat in olive oil. Put in large casserole dish.
  6. Chop the block of American cheese into cubes.
  7. Slice butter into small pieces and put in a warm sauce pan. Melt butter but do not let the butter separate. Whisk it briskly.
  8. Add flour to the butter, continue to whisk. Cook on medium high until it turns brown/blond and smells nutty.
  9. Add 3 cups of milk, continue to whisk until smooth.
  10. Allow bubbles to form in the sauce then add American cheese cubes.
  11. Reduce to medium and add Texas Pete. Then incorporate until sauce is smooth again.
  12. Add shredded Cheddar and Jack cheese. Then incorporate until sauce is smooth again.
  13. Pour 4/5 of the sauce on the macaroni and mix. Use the remaining sauce to cover the top.
  14. Add Frosted Flakes to the top of the macaroni.
  15. Bake in the oven for 18-25 minutes or until the Frosted Flakes are carmelized.
