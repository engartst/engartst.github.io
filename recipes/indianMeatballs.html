Indian Meatballs

Original Recipe by Cheryl Malik for 40 Aprons

Ingredients (we eyeball it):

Meatballs:

  • 1 pound ground meat (our favorite is turkey)
  • 2 cups shredded zucchini (3ish zucchini)
  • 2 cloves garlic minced
  • 2 teaspoons fresh ginger grated
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro chopped
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons of garam masala
  • 1 teasoon of salt
  • Coconut oil or ghee for frying

Sauce:

  • 1/4 cup of ghee
  • 2 teaspoons garam masala
  • 2 teaspoons paprika
  • 2 teaspoons ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon (or more) cayenne pepper
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger grated
  • 1 8-ounce can tomato sauce
  • 1 tablespoon coconut aminos/soy sauce
  • 2 cans coconut cream (get at Sprouts or TJ's)
  • Juice of one lemon
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon of salt

Instructions

  1. Combine all meatball ingredients in a large bowl. Roll uniform balls.
  2. Heat a thin layer of coconut oil or ghee in a large skillet or dutch oven; medium heat. Working in batches, fry meatballs, taking care not to overcrowd, until they are brown on the outside. Transfer them to plate-- no need to cook all the way through.
  3. When meatballs are cooked, clean out skillet. Add 1/4 ghee to skillet/dutch oven and melt over medium-low heat. Add garam masale, paprika, coriander, cayenne pepper, and ginger. Stir fry 1 minute until fragrant and a smooth, incorporated paste.
  4. Increase to medium heat and add tomato sauce and coconut aminos/soy sauce. Simmer for 5 minutes or until sauce has thickened, stir occasionally.
  5. Add coconut cream, lemon juice, and meatballs and simmer for 5-10 minutes or until sauce has thickened slightly and meatballs are cooked through. Add plenty of salt to taste.
  6. Serve with rice/cauliflower rice and chopped cilantro.
