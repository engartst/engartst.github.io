# Miso-Lemon Vinaigrette

*[Original Recipe from Brandon Matzek of Kitchen Konfidence](https://www.kitchenkonfidence.com/2015/03/kale-salad-with-miso-lemon-vinaigrette)*

## **Ingredients (we double the dressing):**

### Dressing:

- 2 tablespoons of white miso (get it at whole foods)
- 1 tablespoon of honey
- 1 tablespoon of seaseme oil
- 2 tablespoons of rice wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons of fresh squeezed lemon juice
- 3 tablespoons of olive oil
- Kosher salt
- Fresh ground black pepper

### Optional Kale Salad Stuff:

- 2 bunches of kale (curly or dino)
- 1 cup of cooked farro or quinoa
- 1/4 cup of unsalted nuts, like walnuts or pistachios

## **Instructions**  

1. Add miso, honey, seasame oil, vinegar, olive oil, and lemon juice to a blender. Blend well and season with salt and pepper.
2. Toss with kale and massage into leaves until the kale breaks down.
3. Add other stuff, except nuts. Add those prior to eating.
