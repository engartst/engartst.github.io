# Poppyseed Cake from the Kitchen of Jody Steren

## Ingredients 

- 1 box of yellow cake mix
- 1 pack of instant vanilla pudding
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup of sour cream
- 1/2 cup poppyseeds
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup cream sherry

## Directions

1. Combine all ingredients in order named. Beat well with an electric mixer for 5 minutes
2. Pour mixture into greased Bundt pan. 
3. Bake 1 hour at 325 degrees. 
4. Remove from pan when cool. Sprinkle with powdered sugar before serving
