About the Conference

Empowering libraries to serve their communities better

Our Regional Library Mini-Conference brings together library professionals, community leaders, and education advocates to share knowledge, discuss challenges, and explore innovative solutions for the future of library services and librarianship.

Network & Connect

Meet fellow library professionals and build lasting connections within our regional community.

Learn & Innovate

Discover cutting-edge practices and innovative approaches to library services and community engagement.

Professional Development

Earn continuing education credits while advancing your professional skills and knowledge.

Featured Speakers

Learn from leading experts in library science and community engagement

Dr. Tracie D. Hall

Keynote Speaker - Former Executive Director, American Library Association

Renowned advocate for intellectual freedom and social justice in libraries. Dr. Hall addresses the challenges facing modern librarians and how to prepare "warrior librarians" for the future.

Michael Chen

Director, Metro Public Library System

Innovative leader in community programming and digital inclusion initiatives across urban library networks.

Lisa Thompson

Youth Services Coordinator

Award-winning children's librarian specializing in early literacy and family engagement programs.

Robert Kim

Technology Integration Specialist

Expert in library automation systems and digital resource management with focus on accessibility.
