# Break, break, break.

## Composed for the UGA Repertory Singers

This piece was composed following the death of the matriarch of my family, my maternal grandmother. The text of the piece is from the Alfred, Lord Tennyson's 1835 poem, "Break, break, break". 

Premeire performance:

- Ramsey Recital Hall, UGA Repertory Singers' Spring Recital, April 13, 2016.

Other performances:

- Dancz Center for New Music, Stewart Engart Masters Recital, November 14, 2016.
