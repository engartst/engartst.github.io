Above Rubies

Commissioned by the Chapel Hill Contemporary Music Ensemble.

The director of the Chapel Hill Contemporary Music Ensemble commissioned me to write a piece for laptop and one of their performers. At the time I was diving head first into ChucK and thought it would be a great chance to experiment with a laptop ensemble.
